# Partner logo sources

Partner artwork from first-party project websites and files supplied by the site owner for the partner ticker.

- Aptos: https://aptosnetwork.com/media-kit — original inline header logo; SVG viewport added for standalone scaling.
- Decibel: https://decibel.trade/logo-wordmark.svg — original wordmark asset.
- Sahara AI: https://saharaai.com/ — header asset https://framerusercontent.com/images/QpHZ5AEzH1KLsUmJHp6Q0gp3M9U.svg.
- Bio Protocol: https://www.bio.xyz/ — original inline header logo; standalone CSS variable resolved to the site's #e5ffd1 brand color.

Logo paths and proportions preserved. CSS displays the logos in monochrome for legibility on the pale ticker background.

- Noctra: original PNG from https://noctra.ai/_next/static/media/img-logo-text-primary.037cb203.png, matching the image supplied by the site owner. Stored unchanged as `dist/assets/partners/noctra.png`; no vector tracing or color filter.

- Kingdom Raids: logo extracted from the site owner’s promotional image with its background and surrounding scene removed. Full transparent edit retained in `assets/partners/kingdom-raids-logo.png`; optimized transparent WebP served from `dist/assets/partners/kingdom-raids.webp`. Original wordmark colors are displayed without the ticker’s monochrome filter.

- KuCoin: original green SVG from the official https://www.kucoin.com/vi header, https://assets.staticimg.com/kc-v2-config/site-config/693bcdd4680db10001fa9856_logo_general_green.svg. Stored unchanged as `dist/assets/partners/kucoin.svg`; official proportions and color preserved.

- BeraPlay and PegaRace: background removed from the two promotional images supplied by the site owner. Transparent full-resolution edits retained in `assets/partners/beraplay-logo.png` and `assets/partners/pegarace-logo.png`; cropped, optimized transparent WebP copies served from `dist/assets/partners/`. Brand colors retained; a subtle shadow keeps the white BeraPlay lettering visible against the pale ticker.

- BingX: original inline SVG from the footer of https://bingx.com/en, decoded without changing its artwork and stored as `dist/assets/partners/bingx.svg`. Transparent monochrome wordmark.

- Bitrue: original transparent PNG from the header of https://www.bitrue.com/, https://static.bitrue.com/img/website/logo_for_next.png. Stored unchanged as `dist/assets/partners/bitrue.png`; displayed with the ticker’s monochrome filter for contrast on the pale background.
